# TA Live Demo Script — Reading Dashboards for Insight
> Session 24 | Runtime: ~8 minutes | Delivered at session end, after Balaji's lecture

**Setup before class starts:** A brand-new, never-before-seen Kirana365 dashboard prepared in advance ("Pune Store — August 2026") that the TA has not discussed with students, so the reading is genuinely live and unrehearsed for them.

---

```
STEP 1 — Orient out loud (≈1 min)
  Action: Open the Pune dashboard, cold
  Say out loud: "I haven't shown you this before. Watch me actually
  do this live. Title says Pune Store, August 2026 — so I know the
  city and the time period before I read a single number."
```

```
STEP 2 — Scan for the biggest element (≈1 min)
  Action: Point at the largest KPI tile: "New Customer Orders: +42%"
  Say out loud: "This is the biggest, boldest thing on the page.
  Whoever built this wants me to notice new customer growth first."
```

```
STEP 3 — Question what it's arguing for (≈1 min)
  Say out loud: "My guess, before I dig further: this dashboard is
  building a case that Pune's new-customer growth is working and
  probably deserves more investment. Let's check that guess."
```

```
STEP 4 — Check trend vs noise on the supporting chart (≈2 min)
  Action: Open the daily new-signups line chart underneath
  Result: a steady 6-week upward climb, not a single spike
  Say out loud: "Good — this isn't one lucky day. Six weeks of
  consistent climb. That's a real trend, not noise."
```

```
STEP 5 — Check the axis before trusting the shape (≈1 min)
  Action: Point at the y-axis starting point
  Result: axis starts at zero, confirming the visual climb is real,
  not exaggerated
  Say out loud: "Axis starts at zero. This steep-looking climb is
  actually steep — not a truncated-axis illusion."
```

```
STEP 6 — Convert to an insight statement (≈2 min)
  Say out loud: "Description: 'new customer orders in Pune rose 42%
  over six weeks.' Insight: 'Pune's new-customer growth has been
  consistently climbing for six straight weeks, not a one-off spike
  — this is strong, sustained evidence that whatever changed there
  in July is genuinely working and worth replicating elsewhere.'"
```

---

> **TA TALKING POINT:** "I just read a dashboard I'd never seen before, in under six minutes, and walked out with a real, usable insight — not because I'm faster at reading numbers than you, but because I followed orient, scan, question, checked the trend, and checked the axis, every single time, in that order."
